### Slash · 杀 · `slash`

Play phase: target one other character within your attack range. The target must use a Dodge or take 1 damage from you. Normally limited to once per Play phase.

## Discrepancies

- **QSanguosha** ([GPL-3.0](https://github.com/Mogara/QSanguosha)) ships a *revised* "standard" package (e.g. Slash ♠5, Peach ♦2, Jink ♦3–♦13 layout) that differs from the 2008 boxed Standard deck. We follow the boxed Standard table (BWIKI / official card list).
- **EX cards** (Frost Blade ♠2, Renwang Shield ♣2, Lightning ♥Q, Negate ♦Q) were shipped in the Standard box as an "EX" supplement; the host can exclude them in the lobby (104-card deck).
